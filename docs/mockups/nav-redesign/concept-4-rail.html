Why a rail

Room to growA vertical list holds six destinations comfortably — plus future ones — with no wrapping or overflow.
GroupingThe rail can label groups (“Spending”, “Accounts”) explicitly, which the pill bar can't.
CostHighest-effort change: a new layout for every page, and the top bar's account controls need rehoming.

On a phone — bottom tab bar

The same five tabs, always in thumb reach. Monthly and Daily keep their existing content, and the Daily entry form is unchanged — it just opens in a “+” modal from any tab.

MobileThe familiar 5-slot bottom bar puts navigation within thumb reach and returns the whole top of the screen to content.
DailyDaily keeps its Today / Plan / Goals tabs, the spends “Show” date filter, per-row delete and the overspend carry-forward prompt. Only the entry form moves into +.
PillsPill management is unchanged: Edit reveals a remove × on each pill, and + Add opens the same “often logged” suggestions plus the “enter your own → Pin it” form.
MonthlyMonthly goes stats-first — net, income/outgoing/savings and category bars. Everything else (transactions, trends, top categories/merchants, anomalies, bills, month actions) moves behind Show more to a side page, one tap away.
LogThe exact current form, unchanged — £ amount, What for? (with title suggestions), When? and Category — just relocated into a “+” sheet that opens from any tab.
SearchSearch becomes a top-bar icon on every screen — most valuable on Monthly, where the six-pill bar used to bury it.

The idea

JobsRename destinations to jobs: “Track” (understand my money — Daily/Monthly/Loans), “Find” (look something up), “Reconcile” (do the monthly task).
CTAReconcile is the only recurrent chore, so it becomes a real button — impossible to forget, and a natural call-to-action from anywhere.
CostNew vocabulary (“Track”, “Find”) must be taught. Renaming Monthly→Track risks losing search-engine/muscle-memory value.

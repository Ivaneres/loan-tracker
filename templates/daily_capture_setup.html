Phone captures

Log Google Wallet taps (and bank app notifications) into Daily automatically. Captures are suggestions — nothing is added to your spending until you confirm it on Daily.

Android only. iPhone has no equivalent hook. Only payments you make with the phone (NFC tap) produce a notification to read.

  1. Turn on Wallet notifications

    Open the Google Wallet app → tap your profile picture → Wallet settings → turn on purchase notifications. Without this, taps produce nothing for the phone to read.

  2. Get your capture token

    ••••••••••••
    {% else %}

    No token is configured on the server yet. Ask whoever runs Tally to set DAILY_CAPTURE_TOKENS for your user, then reload this page.

    {% endif %}

  3. Install MacroDroid and add a macro

    MacroDroid (free) or Tasker both work. Create one macro:

    • Trigger: Notification Received → choose the Google Wallet app. (Add another trigger per bank app later.)
    • Extract the amount and merchant from the notification text with a regular expression (Set Variable → Regular expression). For You paid £12.34 at TESCO STORES with your Visa •••• 1234: 
    amount:    £([0-9]+\.[0-9]{2})      →  $1
merchant:  at (.+?) with            →  $1
    • Action: HTTP Request — method POST, this URL: 
    {{ capture_url }}

    Headers:

    Authorization: Bearer <your token>
Content-Type: application/json

    Body (JSON) — swap in the variables from your regex:

    {
  "amount": "{amount}",
  "title": "{merchant}",
  "source": "wallet",
  "captured_at": "{current_time}"
}

    Variable names depend on MacroDroid/Tasker; use whatever you named them. {captured_at} is display-only. Adding "capture_id" makes retries safe (the same tap won't be added twice).

  4. Test it

    Send a test capture from here, then open Daily — it should appear in the Captured from your phone panel.

    Or from a terminal to check the token:

    curl -X POST {{ capture_url }} \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your token>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount":12.34,"title":"TESCO STORES","source":"wallet"}'

    A reply of {"ok":true,...} means it worked. 401 means the token is wrong; 429 means you're sending too fast.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing appears after a tap. Check purchase notifications are on in Google Wallet, and that the macro's trigger app is Google Wallet (not Google Pay).
  • Amount is wrong or the merchant is blank. The regex didn't match the notification wording; notifications differ by app and language. Tune the pattern.
  • Works sometimes. Android battery optimisation can stop the macro. Allow MacroDroid to run in the background.
  • Missed while offline. The phone is off or offline — those taps are simply lost. Manual entry stays the fallback.
  • See also: docs/daily-capture.md in the Tally repo for the full technical recipe.
